# 04 询盘回复｜两封（普通询价 + 异议）

**填写说明：** 第 8 天写第一封，第 9 天写第二封。英文可直接改名字发出。中文是结构说明，发给客户时不要带中文标题。

**回信顺序（每封都按这个，不要先报一长串工厂介绍）：**  
确认需求 → 只推荐 1 个配置 → 报价 / 附件 → 问缺口信息 → 下一步（样品或 PI）。

---

## 买家来信设定

**第一封（普通询价）：**

```
Hi, we import kitchen tools for supermarkets and foodservice in North Germany.
Please quote 6pcs stainless steel utensil set, private label, trial container.
Need food contact for Germany. Neutral box is OK.
```

**第二封（异议，可当作第一封之后的回信）：**

```
Thanks. Your price is about 8% too high versus other offers from China.
Please send free samples by DHL. Can you do DDP Hamburg warehouse?
Also we need goods in 15 days, not 30.
```

---

## 第一封：普通询价（第 8 天）

**结构：** 点名德国食品接触和贴牌 → 只推 304 砂光套装 → CIF + FOB → 问彩盒和托盘。不要在第一封推销 201、不要写公司成立年份。

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for reaching out. For supermarket and foodservice in Germany, I would quote a 6-piece SUS304 set with satin finish, not 201 — it holds up better in dishwashers and with acidic food.

Item KS-304-6: slotted turner, solid turner, ladle, skimmer, pasta server, slotted spoon. Neutral color box, your sticker or printed logo. We can arrange LFGB testing on this item and send the English report before shipment.

Trial container (1 x 40'HQ, qty to be confirmed with carton size):
  USD 6.35 / set  CIF Hamburg
  USD 6.10 / set  FOB Ningbo

Payment: 30% T/T down, 70% T/T against copy of B/L
Lead time: 30 days after deposit and artwork confirmed
Offer valid 7 days

Could you confirm two points so I can lock packing:
1) Sticker on a stock-style neutral box, or a printed box (MOQ 3,000)?
2) Palletized or floor loaded?

I can send two sets for you to check weight and finish. Freight can be collect, or I quote DHL separately; the sample charge can come off the bulk order.

Best regards,
[Your name]
```

**常见外行句，不要用：**

- We are a leading manufacturer with 20 years experience.
- We can supply all kinds of kitchenware at best price.
- Looking forward to your kind reply. （可留一句短的，但不要空话堆结尾）
- 第一封就报 6 个货号让客户自己挑。

---

## 第二封：砍价 8% + 免费样品 + DDP + 15 天交期（第 9 天）

**处理原则：**

| 对方要什么 | 你怎么回（业务上） |
| --- | --- |
| 便宜 8% | 可以靠拢，但锁整柜、贴纸盒、不免费国际快递；不降到 201 |
| 免费样品 | 货费可免或象征性收，**运费不扛**；大货扣除 |
| DDP | 不报门口价；请他们用货代；坚持则要 EORI + 邮编再另算 |
| 15 天 | 304 + 彩盒做不到就直说；现货多半是 201 或旧包装，不拿这个冒充 |

```
Hi [Name],

Understood on price, samples, delivery, and DDP. Straight answers below.

Price
USD 6.35 CIF Hamburg can move to USD 5.95 / set CIF Hamburg if we keep SUS304 and the 1.0 mm working end, you take the full 40'HQ, and we use sticker logo on the neutral box (no new printed box). I would not drop another step by switching to 201 — that is the usual way Chinese offers get 8% cheaper, and it is the wrong spec for your market.

Samples
I can send 2 sets. I will waive the unit charge; please take DHL freight collect, or I can prepay and add the actual freight on the invoice. If you place the container, I deduct the sample value.

DDP
I will not quote DDP from here. Duty, 19% VAT, destination charges and last-mile are on the import side and I do not have your EORI or warehouse postcode. Please compare my CIF Hamburg with your forwarder. If you still want DDP later, send EORI + delivery postcode and we price it as a separate line with a buffer — I will not guess a door price.

Lead time
15 days is not realistic for 304 with confirmed artwork. We can ship in 30 days after deposit and box confirmation. 15-day cargo is usually stock 201 or mixed leftover boxes. I would rather lose the point than ship that as your first container.

If USD 5.95 CIF Hamburg + sticker box + 30 days works, I will send the PI today.

Best regards,
[Your name]
```

**5.95 从哪来：** `02` 里 8% off 6.35 是 5.84；这里对外收到 **5.95**，用贴纸盒换条件，比把利润打到极限更像老业务。核算表若只演示 5.84，面试就说：对外我收到 5.95，再往下要动规格。第 13 天把 `02` 备忘和这封信的 5.95 写在一起，避免两套数。

**可选一句（对方继续要 DDP 时再发，不必第一轮就写长）：**

```
If it helps your comparison: CIF Hamburg means I cover ocean freight and insurance to Hamburg port. Your forwarder handles discharge, customs, VAT and trucking. That split is how most of our EU importers work on the first container.
```
